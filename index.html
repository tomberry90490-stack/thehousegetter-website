Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do you determine your cash offer?

We look at your home's location, condition, recent comparable sales in the area, and the cost of any repairs needed. We then present a fair, written offer and walk you through the numbers.

What areas do you buy houses in?

We buy houses throughout Washington, DC and Maryland — including Prince George's County, Montgomery County, Baltimore and the surrounding areas. Not sure if we cover your property? Just ask us.

Are there any fees or commissions?

No. There are no agent commissions and no fees to you. We also pay the standard closing costs. The amount we offer is the amount you walk away with (minus any existing mortgage or liens on the property).

Do I have to make repairs or clean the house?

Not at all. We buy houses as-is — you can even leave behind any items you don't want to take with you.

How fast can you close?

It depends on the title work, but we can often close in as little as 7–14 days. If you need more time, that's fine too — you choose the closing date.

Am I obligated to accept your offer?

No. Our offer is 100% free and with no obligation. If it isn't right for you, you simply say no.

Are you real estate agents?

No. We are real estate investors who buy properties directly. We don't list homes and we don't charge commissions. In some cases, we may partner with or assign our purchase contract to another investor, and we will always be upfront with you about that.

Where does the closing happen?

Every closing is handled by a licensed, independent title company or real estate attorney. They make sure the title is clear and that you receive your funds securely.
